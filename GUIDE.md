Welcome to your **26-Year Zerodha Kite + Console Historical Simulator**. This guide explains every screen, button, order type, charge, and strategy in plain English so you can master investing and trading with **₹2,00,000 virtual capital** before risking real money on Zerodha.

## 1. How the Simulator Works (Big Picture)

1. **Pick a Past Date (`2000–2026`) & Capital (Default `₹2,00,000`):** Travel back to any market day or famous era (*2003 Bull Run*, *2008 Financial Crisis*, *2014 Election Rally*, *2020 COVID Crash*).
2. **Zero Future Peek & Real Unadjusted Prices:** Every chart, 52-week high/low, and share price is strictly cut off at your simulated date. Stocks display their *actual* historical Rupee price on that day — when you fast-forward across a **Stock Split** date, your Demat share count automatically multiplies, and crossing a **Dividend** date deposits real cash dividends into your **Funds** balance!
3. **Fast-Forward or Trade Intraday:** Step forward `+1 Day`, `+1 Wk`, `+1 Mo`, `+6 Mo`, `+1 Yr` (or press `Auto-Play`) to experience years of compounding in minutes, or switch to **`Intraday Live (5m)`** to replay 5-minute candles from `9:15 AM` to `3:30 PM IST`.

## 3. Beginner's Guide to the Zerodha Order Window

### Product Types
* **`Longterm — CNC` (1x Cash & Carry):** **Start here!** Pay 100% cash, ₹0 Zerodha brokerage, zero borrowing, hold forever in **Holdings**.
* **`Intraday — MIS` (5x Leverage):** Pay 20% margin, trade 5x value (buy or short-sell), **must close same day**. Open `MIS` trades auto-square-off at **3:20 PM IST** (`₹50 + GST` fee).
* **`Margin — MTF` (4x Delivery Margin):** Pay 25% cash, borrow 75% from Zerodha at `0.04%/day (~14.6%/yr)` interest.
* **`Overnight — NRML`:** Hold monthly Futures & Options (`CE` / `PE`) contracts up to expiry.

### Order Types
* **`Market`:** Instant buy/sell at current market price (`LTP`).
* **`Limit`:** Buy/sell only at your chosen price or better.
* **`SL` / `SL-M`:** Automatic Stop-Loss orders triggered if price moves against you.

## 4. Exact Zerodha Charges & Taxes Simulated
* **Equity Delivery (`CNC`):** `₹0` brokerage + `0.1%` STT + `0.015%` Stamp Duty + Exchange/SEBI/GST + **`₹15.34` CDSL DP Charge** once per stock per day on CNC sell.
* **Equity Intraday (`MIS`):** `0.03%` or `₹20` (lower) per order + `0.025%` STT on Sell.
* **F&O (`NRML` / `MIS`):** Flat `₹20` per order + statutory STT/Exchange/GST.
* **Console Tax Estimate:** `20%` STCG (held ≤ 12 months) and `12.5%` LTCG above `₹1.25 Lakh` exemption (held > 12 months).
